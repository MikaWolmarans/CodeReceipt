Features

Understand

Plain English Breakdowns

Every component, route, and function explained in plain language. No jargon. Written for the person who shipped it, not who wrote it.

Navigate

Architecture Map

A written map of how your app is structured — frontend, backend, database, APIs. See the whole car before you open the hood.

Maintain

Maintenance Guides

Flagged areas that need attention. How to add features safely. What to avoid touching. Think of it as the service schedule for your codebase.

Learn

Concept Explanations

Not just what your code does — but why. We surface the patterns your AI used so you actually level up while you read.

Privacy

Zero Retention

Your source code is never stored, logged, or used for training. Processed ephemerally. Gone the moment your manual is ready.

Format

Print-Ready PDF

Beautifully typeset. Indexed. Sectioned. The kind of document you could actually put on a shelf. Because some things are better offline.
